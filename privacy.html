Nerd Tips - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy applies to the Nerd Tips Android app (com.nerdtips.nerd_tips), published by Mark_Tech ("we", "us").

What the app does

Nerd Tips shows short tech tips on AI, software, security, mobile and developer tools. The app has no accounts, and we do not ask you for your name, email or any other personal information.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information ourselves. The app stores your settings (such as your preferences) only on your device.

Ads and third-party services

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use certain information to show ads and measure their performance, including your device's advertising ID, approximate location, app interactions, and crash and diagnostic data. This is handled under Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device's Settings (Google, then Ads).

Links

Some tips contain links to other websites or app stores. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
